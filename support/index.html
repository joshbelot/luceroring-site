Lucero support

Lucero is made by Josh Belot. For help, write to support@luceroring.com and say what you were doing. Include the version shown at the bottom of Help (Ring tab, Help, About). Lucero is for general wellness. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything.

Quick links: Pairing · Charging · Fit · Syncing · Backups · Contact

Pairing

  1. Charge the ring for about 20 minutes first, then put it on.
  2. Open Lucero, tap Set up my ring, then My ring is on.
  3. Make sure Bluetooth is on, keep your phone close, and tap Scan for rings. Rings show up in bursts, so this can take up to a minute.
  4. Pick your ring from the list. The first time, iOS asks to let Lucero use Bluetooth. Tap Allow.

If your ring doesn't show up, try these in order:

  • Close any other app made for your ring. A ring talks to one app at a time.
  • Put the ring on its charger for ten seconds, then take it off again to wake it.
  • Keep it close to your phone while it connects.

No ring yet? On the setup screen, tap Explore with sample data to look around with made-up data. Tap End in the banner when you're done.

Charging

  • Put the ring on its charger and leave it until it's full. A first charge of about 20 minutes is enough to start.
  • Battery usually lasts several days. Frequent heart rate and oxygen checks use more.
  • The Ring tab shows the battery level, and an estimate once Lucero has seen a couple of charges.
  • If your ring is getting low, Lucero can remind you to charge it before bed, so tonight's sleep is recorded.

Fit

  • Wear the ring snug, not tight. It shouldn't spin, and it shouldn't leave a mark.
  • Turn the ring so the sensor bumps sit against your palm.
  • Best on the index or middle finger of your non-dominant hand, because that finger moves least when your hands are busy.
  • To find the finger that gives the steadiest readings, use Find my best finger (Ring tab).

Syncing

  • Open Lucero to sync. On Today, pull down to sync again.
  • Your ring only remembers a limited amount of time (about a week). If Lucero doesn't sync for longer than that, the days in between are gone for good. Open Lucero and sync at least once a week.
  • If the ring won't connect or sync, follow the steps under Pairing, then look at Advanced (troubleshooting) at the bottom of the Ring tab. It holds connection tools, sync settings and logs, and reports you can send to support. Nothing there is needed day to day.
  • A ring on a finger usually counts fewer steps than a phone in your pocket. Lucero scales the ring's count up a little and, if you allow it, tunes it to you over time, by no more than 5% a week. You can see and reset this under What Lucero learned.

Backups

Everything Lucero knows is stored on your phone only. To keep a copy:

  • Automatic backup (Ring tab, Backup): choose a folder, and Lucero saves a file there at most once a day, after the day's first sync. It keeps 7 daily files and one for each of the last 4 weeks. The file names start with Lucero-backup-. A banner appears if no backup has worked for 3 days.
  • Export (Ring tab): makes a file you can save or share.
  • Restore: use Import on the Ring tab and pick a backup file.

Backups and exports leave out anything that came from Apple Health. After a restore, Lucero reads that from Health again.

To delete everything, open the Ring tab, then Privacy, then Delete all data.

Contact

support@luceroring.com. Say what you were doing and which ring you have.

In the app, Send feedback (Ring tab, Help) opens a message in your own mail app. It's sent only if you press Send. You can attach a diagnostics file, which is off unless you turn it on, and you can read it before you send it.
