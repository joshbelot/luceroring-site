Lucero privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Lucero is made by Josh Belot.

The short version. Lucero keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't track you. Josh Belot never receives your health data.

What Lucero is

Lucero is an iPhone app for a Lucero-compatible smart ring. It reads sleep, heart rate, temperature, steps and similar readings from your ring over Bluetooth and turns them into plain-language views of your sleep, energy and stress. Lucero is for general wellness. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything.

What Lucero processes, and where it stays

Lucero processes:

  • the readings your ring records (sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, temperature, steps, stress readings and the ring's battery level);
  • what you tell it: your profile (age, sex, height and weight, which the ring needs to work out calories), workouts you log, tags, morning check-ins, and period days if you turn on cycle tracking;
  • the settings you choose in the app.

All of this is stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. The summary on Today is written on your phone too. None of it is sent to Josh Belot or to any other company, because Lucero has no server to send it to. Lucero contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind, and has no account or sign-in.

What Lucero asks your phone for, and when

Bluetooth. Used to find, pair with and sync your ring, and to stay connected to it. Lucero talks only to the ring you pair. It doesn't use Bluetooth for anything else.

Apple Health (optional). You choose in the iOS Health permission sheet which types Lucero may use, and you can change that at any time in Health. If you allow it:

  • Lucero reads your logged workouts (so a hard session isn't mistaken for stress), your body weight, your period days, and the caffeine and alcohol you logged. It offers these to you, and nothing is applied without your tap.
  • Lucero writes to Health your ring's sleep, heart rate and body temperature, your workouts with their heart rate, active energy and distance, the period days you log in Lucero, and each breathing session as a mindful session.

Health data is not sent anywhere by Lucero. Lucero doesn't use Health data for advertising, and doesn't give it to data brokers or other third parties. Lucero doesn't store Health data in iCloud, and Lucero's backups and exports leave out everything that came from Health.

Motion and fitness. If you allow it, Lucero counts steps and distance with your phone's motion sensor during a live workout or a walk test, and can compare them with your ring's step count to tune it to you. It looks at step counts only, and only on your phone.

Location (while you use the app). Lucero can remember where your phone was when it last heard your ring, to help you find it. If you turn on Use GPS, it also records the route of a workout while the live workout screen is open. Locations stay on your phone. Lucero doesn't use location in the background.

Microphone. Only when you start a snoring session. Your phone listens overnight and works out, minute by minute, whether it heard snoring. No audio is saved or sent. Lucero keeps only the minute-by-minute results. The microphone isn't used at any other time. You can stop a session whenever you like; it also stops about 45 minutes after your ring says you woke, or after 12 hours.

Notifications. Reminders and summaries (for example a morning summary or a low ring battery) are created on your phone. Nothing is pushed from a server.

Backups and exports

You decide where your data goes.

  • Automatic backup is off until you turn it on. You choose a folder in the system file picker, and Lucero writes a file there (named Lucero-backup- and the date, ending in .json), at most once a day. It keeps the last 7 daily files and one for each of the last 4 weeks. Lucero deletes only files with that name. Lucero doesn't use iCloud itself; if the folder you pick is in a cloud service, that service's rules apply.
  • Export makes a file you can save or share with the iOS share sheet.
  • Backups and exports contain ring data and what you entered. They leave out everything that came from Apple Health (workouts, weight, period days, and caffeine and alcohol read from Health). After a restore, Lucero reads those from Health again.

Josh Belot never receives these files.

Send feedback

Send feedback (in Help and in Privacy) opens a message in your own mail app, addressed to support@luceroring.com. It's sent only if you press Send. The message includes your app version. You can also turn on Attach a diagnostics file, which is off unless you turn it on, and you can read the file before you send it. The file has the app and iOS version, your phone model, your ring model, firmware and supported features, the timing of the last five syncs, the step-tuning factor, a few internal status values, summaries of recent app crashes and freezes, and counts of how many days of data you have. It has no health readings, names, birth dates or locations. If you send a message, the support mailbox receives your email address and what you wrote, and we use them only to answer you.

Tracking, accounts and servers

Lucero doesn't track you, within the meaning Apple gives that word, and doesn't link your data with other companies' data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. It doesn't contact the internet itself. The data it holds is on your phone only.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the Ring tab, then Privacy, then Delete all data. It asks twice, because it can't be undone. It removes your history, your settings and your ring from the phone and clears Lucero's files, and you can choose to also remove what Lucero saved to Health. Deleting the app removes its storage as well. Files you exported or backed up are yours; delete them where you saved them.

Children

Lucero isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone. Because nothing leaves the phone, there is nothing to collect.

Changes to this policy
